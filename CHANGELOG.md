# Schémo updates

Each update is named **week.number**: `3.2` is the 2nd update of week 3.
Weeks are 7-day periods counted from the day Schémo was created (week 1 = October 29 to November 4, 2026).

## 1.4 (2026-11-01)
- **Real installer**: `Schemo-Setup.exe` replaces `Installer-Schemo.cmd`. You choose the folder, Schémo can be pinned to the taskbar, and it uninstalls from Windows Settings. The desktop shortcut is a checkbox on the last screen.
- **In-app updates**: Schémo offers new versions when it starts, or through File → Check for updates. If you have an older version, install the Setup once by hand.
- **Dates**: `03/2026`, `15/03`, `mai 68` and `5 000 av. J.-C.` now sort correctly.
- Ctrl+S, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+F and F1 also work when the cursor is in a panel field.
- Text on collapsed frames follows the chosen language.

## 1.3 (2026-11-01)
- **Timeline**: new shape, and "New timeline sheet" in the File menu. Boxes follow a time line and sort themselves by date, written freely
  (2026-03-15, March 2026, Q1 2026, 1967, Day 3…). Horizontal or vertical.
- **Periods**: "from 2000 to 2010", "2000-2010", "3 to 15 March 2026", "March to June 2026", "between 2000 and 2010",
  "since 2010", "1990s", "20th century". A band shows them on the line, and the Date field tells you whether Schémo understood.

## 1.2 (2026-11-01)
- **Arrows with frames**: an arrow can now link a frame to a box, a box to a frame, or two frames.
  Click a frame's title bar to see its **+**. A frame can't be linked to what it contains.

## 1.1 (2026-11-01)
- **Markdown description**: the "Sub-points" field becomes a description under the box title
  (bold, italic, code, strikethrough, headings, lists, checkboxes, quotes, lines). Sub-points from older files become a list.
- Numbered updates. The number appears in the File menu and in the help.

## Before numbering (October 29 to November 1, 2026)
- First version: boxes, arrows, frames and tabs; automatic layout; PNG, PDF and SVG export.
- French and English interface.
- Editable gradients and colors: for the whole tab or box by box, including text and border.
