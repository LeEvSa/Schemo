# Schémo updates

Each update is named **week.number**: `3.2` is the 2nd update of week 3.
Weeks are 7-day periods counted from the day Schémo was created (week 1 = October 29 to November 4, 2026).

## 3.7 (2026-11-16)
- **Timeline to scale**: with nothing selected, the panel's Time line section switches between Even and To scale. To scale, the gap between boxes follows time, marks (years, months, days or hours) appear on the line, and boxes too close together move to another row. The unit is automatic or chosen (Hours to Centuries).

## 3.6 (2026-11-16)
- **Target** shape: circles inside each other. The core sits in the center, what is linked to it on the 1st circle, what is linked to those on the 2nd, and so on; unlinked boxes go on a last, dashed circle.

## 3.5 (2026-11-16)
- **Family tree** shape: a line without arrowheads joins a couple, side by side; an arrow goes from a parent to their child, and children sit under the middle of the couple. One color per generation. The + adds a child, a parent or a partner.

## 3.4 (2026-11-16)
- **Board** shape: one frame per act or chapter, as columns side by side, with scenes stacked inside. Drag a scene into another column to change its chapter.

## 3.3 (2026-11-16)
- **Story arc** shape: scenes follow a curve that shows the tension of the story. Each scene has a Tension level (1 to 5) in the panel; a scene left on Auto sits between its neighbors, and with no level at all the arc takes the classic shape.

## 3.2 (2026-11-16)
- **Network** shape: characters and relationships laid out as a web, linked boxes close together, with no top or bottom. New links are lines without arrowheads; adding a character barely moves the others.

## 3.1 (2026-11-16)
- **Story threads** shape: one frame per character or plot line, stacked as bands. Scenes run left to right and line up in time across bands; an arrow from one band to another pushes the scene after it. The + adds a scene before or after, without an arrow.

## 2.2 (2026-11-09)
- **Dark background for exports**: PNG, SVG and PDF can be exported on a dark background (the same as the canvas in dark mode). Arrows, frames and the timeline then take their dark-mode colors; boxes keep theirs. PNG and SVG keep the white and transparent choices.

## 2.1 (2026-11-07)
- **Color picker**: Schémo has its own color picker. It opens on the HEX code, and remembers the mode you choose (HEX, RGB or HSL) and your recent colors. Enter or a click elsewhere keeps the color; Esc goes back to the starting color.
- **Folders**: Open, Save as and Export each remember the last folder you used. Save as starts in the diagram's own folder when it already has a file.

## 1.4 (2026-11-01)
- **Real installer**: `Schemo-Setup.exe` replaces `Installer-Schemo.cmd`. You choose the folder, Schémo can be pinned to the taskbar, and it uninstalls from Windows Settings. The desktop shortcut is a checkbox on the last screen.
- **In-app updates**: Schémo offers new versions when it starts, or through File → Check for updates. If you have an older version, install the Setup once by hand.
- **Dates**: `03/2026`, `15/03`, `mai 68` and `5 000 av. J.-C.` now sort correctly.
- Ctrl+S, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+F and F1 also work when the cursor is in a panel field.
- Text on collapsed frames follows the chosen language.

## 1.3 (2026-11-01)
- **Timeline**: new shape, and "New timeline sheet" in the File menu. Boxes follow a time line and sort themselves by date, written freely
  (2026-03-15, March 2026, Q1 2026, 1967, Day 3…). Horizontal or vertical.
- **Periods**: "from 2000 to 2010", "2000-2010", "3 to 15 March 2026", "March to June 2026", "between 2000 and 2010",
  "since 2010", "1990s", "20th century". A band shows them on the line, and the Date field tells you whether Schémo understood.

## 1.2 (2026-11-01)
- **Arrows with frames**: an arrow can now link a frame to a box, a box to a frame, or two frames.
  Click a frame's title bar to see its **+**. A frame can't be linked to what it contains.

## 1.1 (2026-11-01)
- **Markdown description**: the "Sub-points" field becomes a description under the box title
  (bold, italic, code, strikethrough, headings, lists, checkboxes, quotes, lines). Sub-points from older files become a list.
- Numbered updates. The number appears in the File menu and in the help.

## Before numbering (October 29 to November 1, 2026)
- First version: boxes, arrows, frames and tabs; automatic layout; PNG, PDF and SVG export.
- French and English interface.
- Editable gradients and colors: for the whole tab or box by box, including text and border.
